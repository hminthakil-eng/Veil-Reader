# Veil Reader — Research Department

## Mission
Reduce product and technical uncertainty before expensive implementation. Research exists to answer the questions that should not be guessed.

## Ownership
Research owns discovery, competitive analysis, UX evidence, technical spikes, prototype studies, benchmark design, accessibility investigation, and evidence-backed recommendations.

Research does not ship production features directly. It produces decisions and evidence that Development can execute.

## Operating lanes
1. Reader UX & accessibility — typography, navigation, RTL/mixed-script behavior, TalkBack, reduced motion, tablet/foldable ergonomics.
2. Reading technology — EPUB/PDF/CBZ/webtoon feasibility, Readium capabilities, pagination, page-turn approaches, rendering edge cases.
3. Product & gamification — Castle progression, mystery systems, Paths, motivation, retention, ethical gamification and distraction risk.
4. Performance — large-library behavior, large PDF/annotation sets, startup, jank, memory, benchmark methodology.
5. Future systems — spoiler-safe book AI, local semantic search, TTS/audiobooks, sync and privacy architecture.

## Required output for every research task
- Question / decision to make
- Why it matters
- Evidence and sources
- Options considered
- Experiment or prototype result when useful
- Recommended decision
- Confidence level
- Risks / unknowns
- Acceptance criteria for Development

## Decision states
- GO — evidence is strong enough to build.
- GO WITH GUARDRAILS — build only within stated constraints.
- SPIKE — Development may create a bounded technical prototype, not production integration.
- HOLD — evidence is insufficient or value is unclear.
- NO-GO — reject or defer the idea.

## First research program
Prioritize questions that directly unblock the v0.9 → 1.0 roadmap: Veil visual system, Castle-map interaction, reader immersion, accessibility, large-library performance, and adaptive layouts. Future AI/social/cloud features remain separate until 1.0 gates are satisfied.
