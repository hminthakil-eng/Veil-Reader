# Veil Reader — RPG Systems Bible v0.1

## Core fantasy
The player is not playing a separate game beside a reader. They are a reader-character whose real reading history becomes the canonical history of a persistent world.

## Character model
- Origin: narrative background chosen at onboarding; affects flavor and initial quest hooks, never reading access.
- Path: primary RPG class. Existing Paths become mechanically distinct classes.
- Rank: major progression tier earned through rituals and evidence-backed milestones.
- Attributes: Insight, Resolve, Memory, Curiosity, Discipline, Imagination.
- Traits: long-lived behavioral identity inferred or selected with explicit user control.
- Chronicle: immutable-style timeline of meaningful reading/world events.

## Progression currency
Avoid one universal XP bar as the only progression. Use multiple meaningful resources:
- Insight — understanding, notes, recall and difficult nonfiction.
- Echoes — completed narrative journeys and rereads.
- Resolve — sustained long-form sessions and difficult completions.
- Curiosity — genre/topic exploration and discovery.
- Sigils — rare milestone rewards used for Path/world unlocks, never sold.

## Path identity
Oracle: deduction, prediction journals, mysteries, rereads and clue work.
Dreamwalker: fantasy/worldbuilding, series exploration, maps and lore.
Archivist: nonfiction, notes, summaries, recall and knowledge graphs.
Vanguard: momentum, long journeys, series completion and challenge arcs.
Nocturne: horror/gothic/dark fantasy, atmosphere, themes and nocturnal journeys without requiring unhealthy schedules.
Artificer: science/technology/hard SF, glossary, concepts and cross-book connections.

## Quest taxonomy
- Main Quest: advances Veil world narrative.
- Reading Quest: grounded in books already in the user's library.
- Path Quest: exercises class-specific reading behaviors.
- Discovery Quest: encourages optional exploration without daily pressure.
- Companion Quest: deepens NPC relationships after meaningful milestones.
- Ritual: rank-up gate entered intentionally by the player.

## World model
Castle is the home hub. It grows into a larger world through unlocked districts, rooms, characters, artifacts and story states. World changes must be explainable by the user's reading history.

Key locations:
- Grand Library — collection and completed journeys.
- Gatehouse — connected manga/source catalogues.
- Guild Hall — factions, NPCs, companion quests.
- Ritual Chamber — Path advancement.
- Observatory — statistics, predictions and reading patterns.
- Hidden Archive — notes, highlights, entities and lore.
- Treasury — earned cosmetics and artifacts.
- Inner Sanctum — mastery/endgame challenges.

## Reading session contract
Active reading is sacred space. No pop-up rewards, combat, dialogue interruption or forced quest UI appears over a book/manga chapter. Rewards resolve when the user exits or intentionally opens the world layer.

## Anti-grind rules
- No reward for rapid page tapping.
- No paid progression.
- No progress loss for missing days.
- No mandatory daily quests.
- No FOMO expirations for core progression.
- Repetition without meaningful reading evidence has diminishing/no reward.

## First design questions
1. Exact attribute formulas and caps.
2. How each Path earns evidence without privileging one format.
3. How manga chapters and books map fairly into progression.
4. Faction/NPC narrative structure and choice persistence.
5. World evolution cadence that feels substantial but not distracting.
6. Quiet Mode behavior for users who want pure reading.
