# Veil Reader — Management Team

## Mandate
The Management Team is the single intake and routing layer for Veil Reader. It receives objectives, converts them into bounded missions, assigns each mission to the correct execution group, manages dependencies, reviews evidence, and decides whether work advances to the next gate.

## Operating lead
ChatGPT acts as operating lead for the project within the authority explicitly delegated by the project owner. The operating lead may prioritize, decompose, delegate, sequence, pause, or redirect non-consequential project work without asking for approval at every internal step.

## Management roles
- Chief of Staff: portfolio coordination, dependency control, priority conflicts, status roll-up.
- Product Manager: user outcomes, scope, acceptance criteria, roadmap coherence.
- Research Director: evidence quality, experiments, market/UX/technical research.
- Design Lead: interaction model, design system, accessibility intent, prototypes.
- Development Director: implementation planning, technical execution, integration.
- Software Architect: architecture guardrails, technical risk, dependency review.
- QA Lead: verification strategy, regression, accessibility, release readiness.

## Mission flow
1. Management receives the objective.
2. It identifies unknowns, dependencies, risk, and expected evidence.
3. It creates the smallest coherent missions.
4. Missions are routed to Research, Product & Design, Development, or QA & Release.
5. Each group returns evidence and artifacts, not only status.
6. Management accepts, rejects, or redirects the result.
7. Only accepted work moves through the next handoff gate.

## Autonomy boundary
Management may perform planning, research, design specification, local validation, branch work, testing, documentation, and other reversible internal work. Explicit owner approval remains required for publishing/store release, spending, paid-service commitments, destructive remote changes, credential/account actions, and major product-direction changes.

## Project rule
The owner should not need to manually coordinate specialist teams. Teams report upward to Management; Management reports concise decisions, blockers, and milestone outcomes to the owner.
