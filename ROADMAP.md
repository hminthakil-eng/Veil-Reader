## Mandatory before/after work protocol

- [Grand Forge Execution Protocol](docs/roadmap/GRAND_FORGE_EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md) — re-check current main and living roadmap BEFORE every stage; record PRE-WORK on an issue; post evidence-linked POST-STAGE after every stage, including failures and rejected work.
- [Grand Forge Stage Receipts](.github/workflows/grand-forge-stage-receipts.yml) — trusted main-only receipt action. [Roadmap Preflight](.github/workflows/grand-forge-roadmap-preflight.yml) checks new PR documentation.
- [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) — mandatory instructions for AI agents, independent QA and staged work.

Stage receipts record source evidence, never independently certify physical or product readiness. Branch protection / required status checks still need administrator configuration.
