# Island Quest and Continuity Room

For each quest write: stable ID, act, NPC, scene, book physical-clue layers, player goal, conflict, funny/tender beat, 3 distinct resolution approaches, coin/material/supplier effects, relationship consequences, state flags, a later callback and accessible recovery. Shop decisions and narrative outcomes must causally intertwine. Story and essential clues never depend on actual EPUB-reading rewards or on violating a character's consent/privacy.

Track knowledge graph: who knows which fact, who physically owns the book/hidden letter, whether reading/disclosure is authorized, what pages were altered, and what NPC believes now. Preserve consistency across romance, quest and economy branches. Prevent dead ends by including at least one alternate route when intimacy is refused.

Write scene graph and state transition first; then Persian dialogue with subtext and distinct voices. Run reachability tests (all intended endings), re-entry/reload tests, missing localization, duplicate reward checks, and 'what if player rejects everyone' scenario. Distinguish proposal from accepted canon.
