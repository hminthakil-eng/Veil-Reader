# NPC Character Voice & Anime VN Dialogue

Every speaking NPC needs: work and money concerns, a private desire not centered on the hero, schedule, meaningful relationship with another NPC, one contradiction, unusual linguistic rhythm, terms they'd never use, and a unique response to the same event.

Each spoken line should change a power balance, expose subtext, reveal personality, invite action or earn a laugh, instead of announcing lore. First passes 8–16 dialogue turns plus scene-stage portrait cues: glance, blink, pose and expressive silence. Portraits high resolution, pixel world separate; RTL text and accessibility must remain readable.

Blind-voice test: remove names from five characters' responses to one scene; an independent critic identifies at least 4 of 5. If not, rewrite. For romance, characters may initiate, negotiate or decline; do not treat a stated 'no' as a puzzle to bypass. The player may pursue intended adult romance candidates through complex authored paths, not gift spam. Maintain beautiful friendship endings.
