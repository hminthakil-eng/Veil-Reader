# Island Romance, Agency and Family Skill

The user wants potential romance routes with all **named adult female romance candidates**, including married women and mothers. Do not exclude a character categorically merely because she is married or has children; however, this is **potential story availability**, not a promise that the protagonist is entitled to affection. Player choices and NPC goals can close a route in a playthrough. NPCs may be faithful, separate of their own initiative, negotiate consensual nonmonogamy, or face consequences for secrecy.

Each route requires: age 18+, existing partner/family/childcare context, independent goals and livelihood, limits/consent, 3 difficult obstacles arising from competing values (not 10 gifts), 2 reciprocal initiatives, 2 meaningful optional endings, friendship route, failure/recovery, relationship flags persisted across scenes, effects on shop and NPC community.

Model relationship as separate availability / compatibility / trust / desire / boundary-respect / active conflict / family wellbeing / social consequences, not affection += 10. Flirtation never overrides refusal, financial debt, job power dynamics or childcare. Children are never romance objects or rewards. No routes for minors, including Bamdad (14). Romantic outcomes do not lock essential island story or reader features.

A married route can be (a) openly consented relationship model, (b) NPC-initiated separation followed by a new partnership, or (c) a difficult secret romance with credible consequences and no glamorized harm. Respect an in-world 'no' and offer a full well-written platonic route. Every major choice changes at least two persistent narrative/economy facts. Test reachability for each intended adult route in at least one coherent story path, hard boundaries, saves, and alternate exits.
