# Veil Reader — Literary Design Reference

Sources reviewed from the user-supplied EPUBs:
- Lord of the Mysteries
- Circle of Inevitability

Purpose: convert literary atmosphere into original UI/UX direction. Do not reproduce prose, illustrations, logos, named symbols, Pathway emblems, Tarot Club identity, exact maps, character designs, or recognizable ritual diagrams.

## Core principle

**Clarity first, mystery second.**

The strongest shared design language is controlled revelation: familiar spaces gaining hidden significance, records becoming meaningful, warm local light against vast darkness, ritual expressed as precise procedure, and architecture carrying memory.

## Motif families

### Fog / liminality
Use layered depth, distant architecture, thresholds, partial reveal and restrained atmospheric obscurity.
Never obscure primary controls or reading text.

### Archival matter
Notebooks, letters, registries, paper, ink, seals and preserved records support ruled entries, folios, archive tabs, provenance and durable-memory metaphors.
Do not make every surface parchment.

### Ritual as procedure
Use sequence, preparation, geometry and consequence for Path/Ritual moments.
Keep ceremonies short, skippable and accessible.
Avoid casino-like reward language and copied occult iconography.

### Industrial / historical precision
Gaslight-era infrastructure, clocks, metalwork, observatories and formal interiors suggest thin brass rails, engraved separators, measured spacing and precise utility controls.
Avoid steampunk cosplay and decorative machinery without function.

### Monumental architecture
Halls, chambers, cathedrals, corridors and hidden structures suggest screens that behave like places rather than dashboards.
Use spatial adjacency on wide layouts.
Do not frame every control.

### Celestial observation
Use sparse astronomical linework, relationship maps, cool moonlit separation and deep blue-black fields.
Avoid bright fantasy starfields and continuous particle motion.

### Mirrors / doubled states
Use subtle before/after, known/unknown, memory-echo and identity metaphors.
Do not literally mirror navigation or reverse semantic behavior.

### Underground / hidden structure
Use recessed advanced layers, deeper archive history and discoverable secondary systems.
Do not hide essential actions.

## Realm mapping

### Threshold
Goal: crossing from ordinary life into a private reading world.
Use one dominant current book, architectural depth, localized warm light, restrained fog and immediate resume.
Avoid dashboard density.

### Sanctuary
Goal: silence, material and concentration.
This is the lowest-density realm. Publication content dominates. Shell chrome disappears when unnecessary.
Keep PAGED / PAPER CURL / SLIDE / SCROLL distinct.
No celestial decoration behind reading text.

### Archive
Goal: memory made durable.
Use records, folios, indexes, source return, provenance and precise metadata hierarchy.
Gallery, Shelves and Index should have distinct spatial grammar.
Never invent historical certainty.

### Castle
Goal: long-term reading world made spatial.
Use rooms, maps, architectural adjacency and subtle environmental progression.
Avoid RPG HUD treatment and feature-locking.

### Ritual
Goal: meaningful transformation.
Use short procedural ceremony, geometric registration and clear before/after state.
No gambling cues.

### Sanctum
Goal: rarity and preserved discovery.
Use museum logic, provenance and few strong objects rather than loot presentation.

### Observatory
Goal: reveal relationships ordinary reading does not expose.
Use sparse nodes, thin connecting lines and source-grounded relationships only.

## Material system

- Deep architecture: near-black blue / charcoal-blue for shell, Archive, Castle, Observatory and Threshold.
- Brass / bronze: thin structural accents, separators, selected state and edge registration — never large gold fills.
- Parchment: current-volume resume, Reader Paper/Sepia and preserved archival documents only.
- Ink: calm legible primary content and record hierarchy.
- Moonlight: sparse cool secondary illumination, not glow around every element.

## Typography

Use editorial literary hierarchy for shell identity, readable prose-like UI text, precise utility sans for controls, and a first-class Persian/Arabic family.

Rules:
- No Latin-style tracking on Arabic-script text.
- Tiny mystical labels are prohibited.
- 200% font scale must remain usable.
- Publication typography stays independent from shell branding.

## Motion grammar

Motion should explain entry, ownership, depth, physical page movement, arrival and reveal.
Prefer short instrument transitions and controlled spatial transitions.
Reduced Motion must preserve all information and state clarity.

## Product tensions to preserve

Veil should keep these opposites alive:
- familiar UI / unfamiliar world
- warm object / cold architecture
- precise controls / mysterious context
- ordinary book / accumulated history
- private reading / vast world
- durable record / uncertain interpretation
- calm surface / deep hidden structure
- material paper / abstract celestial mapping

## Non-negotiables

Preserve:
- Readium / Pdfium ownership
- sole TTS architecture
- durable locator/progress semantics
- Room integrity
- RTL/Persian behavior
- accessibility
- PAGED / PAPER CURL / SLIDE / SCROLL separation
- atomic Notes
- Focus Guide
- Lookup
- ETA
- canonical convergence branch history

Prefer refinement over replacement.
Do not claim visual completion without actual Android screenshots and device review.

## One-sentence art direction

**Veil Reader is a quiet private archive suspended between a gas-lit historical institution, a celestial observatory, and a physical reading desk — where every layer of ornament exists to reveal memory, material, or meaning.**
