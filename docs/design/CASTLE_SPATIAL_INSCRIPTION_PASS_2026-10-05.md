# Castle Spatial Inscription Pass — 2026-10-05

## Audit result

The current CastleWorldMap already behaves as architecture:
- continuous floor datum;
- alternating chamber placement;
- bridge/corridor ownership;
- vaulted portals;
- background structural lines;
- narrow-layout stair/corridor adaptation.

A wholesale redesign would duplicate good work.

## Remaining panel problem

Two transient informational surfaces still read as dashboard cards:
- world mutation signal;
- ritual aftermath.

## Change

- remove filled/bordered card treatment from those two surfaces;
- mutation becomes a vertical architectural inscription;
- ritual aftermath becomes a short luminous registration line plus text;
- final growth-note divider becomes a short registration mark instead of a full-width section separator.

## Preserved

- Castle map structure;
- room routing;
- unlock/progression truth;
- world mutation truth;
- ritual timing/state;
- accessibility text;
- no persistence or gamification rule change.

## Intent

Information should appear *inside the place*, not as panels layered on top of it.
