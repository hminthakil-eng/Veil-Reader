## Evidence snapshots (public reception, not revenue)
- Steam page for Stardew Valley displayed ~98% positive English reviews across ~394k entries when checked in October 2026.
- Fields of Mistria displayed ~97% positive English reviews across ~24.8k entries (full release page dates August 5, 2026).
- Tiny Bookshop displayed ~97% positive English reviews across ~5k entries.
- DAVE THE DIVER displayed ~96% positive English reviews across ~51k entries.
- Coffee Talk displayed ~94% positive English reviews across ~4.7k entries.
- Eastward displayed ~85% positive English reviews across ~3.6k entries.
- These observed counts change over time; ratings are not direct proof of game design quality or commercial profitability.

## Research order and method
**Deep-dive 1: Fields of Mistria** — Observe 4 portrait emotions, 3 NPC schedules, 1 relationship quest, one town scene. Record transitions, camera/palette, NPC chatter, menus, daily variations.

**Deep-dive 2: Eastward** — Map building/foreground/background tiles, sprite detail, event/cutscene triggers, dialogue pacing, environmental narrative. Avoid matching original assets.

**Deep-dive 3: Moonlighter** — Stock/pricing feedback loop, customer behavior, day-end analytics, economy exploits, grind risk; adapt to book condition, genres and collector demand.

**Deep-dive 4: Tiny Bookshop** — Customer genre intent, recommendation outcomes, stocking and geographic demand; focus on what ours must do differently (walkable world + quest mystery).

**Deep-dive 5: Coffee Talk / VA-11 Hall-A** — High-quality readable dialogue, distinct voices and repeated returning characters; service choice as narrative branch instead of arbitrary yes/no.

**Deep-dive 6: DAVE THE DIVER** — Carefully study gating and interleaved exploration/management; avoid spreading scope across too many minigames.

Record for each: 10-20 minutes lawful gameplay observation (or official gameplay videos), exact action/state/feedback timeline, screenshot references (link not redistribution), strengths, friction, own non-infringing feature concept, cost estimate, acceptance test, license provenance.

## Art target (composed, not copied)
- World: polished handcrafted pixel-art towns with selective atmospheric lighting, foreground parallax, rain/fog and material cues. Aim for the *readability* of Fields of Mistria and *environmental character* of Eastward.
- Dialogue: sharp anime portraits at high native resolution, subtle eye blink, breath, lip/pose swap; portraits are NOT pixel-scaled world sprites.
- Relationships: original named characters with motives, schedules, personal quest arcs, reactions to shop/region changes, condition-based dialogue.
- Content standards: no random fetch-quest spam. Each mini quest changes at least one relationship, location, business variable, knowledge flag, or future event.
- Quest density goal is a **candidate planning metric**, not a promise: first village prototype 8-12 authored micro quests with 2-4 consequence patterns. Final game scale budget to be determined from measured production throughput.

## Reverse-engineering procedure (clean-room)
1. Only lawful/demo/open-source gameplay and allowed public documentation.
2. Describe observed inputs, visible states, timing and outcomes; avoid dumping proprietary files, circumventing protection, or cracked distributions.
3. Independent implementation from behavioral spec; compare look/feel but do not replicate copyrighted art, source, proprietary scripts, licensed audio or brands.
4. Legal provenance for every reused asset/plugin; check modding/EULA permissions. Use REA tooling only when verified and permitted.
5. Mark each detail OBSERVED, DOCUMENTED, INFERRED, or EXPERIMENTAL.
